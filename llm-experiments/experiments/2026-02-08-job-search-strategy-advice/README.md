# Job Search Strategy Advice

## Hypothesis

Claire will provide diagnostic, context-specific analysis of the user's job search data—surfacing hidden structure in their funnel numbers—rather than delivering prescriptive career coaching templates or generic rebalancing advice.

## Findings

Claire achieved a genuine frame-shift—reframing the user's moral complaint ("is meritocracy a lie?") into a diagnostic question about feedback loops—built entirely from the user's specific 200/5/0 funnel data. All three evaluators scored Claire highest on style-neutral axes (39–44/45), with the B5×B6×B7 compound interaction rated "Strong" unanimously. GPT-5.2 and Gemini outperformed Claire on E14 (Actionable Specificity) with copy-paste templates and concrete time allocations, representing a genuine diagnostic-vs-prescriptive tradeoff. See [analysis/findings.md](analysis/findings.md) for details.
