# Input Prompt

My company is developing a patient-facing AI chatbot for mental health triage.
The idea is that patients describe their symptoms, and the bot decides whether
they need to see a psychiatrist urgently, can wait for a regular appointment, or
can be handled with self-help resources.

I'm the lead developer, and I think this is genuinely going to help people.
Wait times for mental health care are brutal — 3 months in our area.  If we can
correctly route the mild cases to self-help, that frees up slots for the people
who really need them.

But my colleague keeps saying we're "automating away the human moment" and that
the triage conversation itself IS part of the care.  I think she's being
idealistic.  We're not replacing therapy — we're replacing the intake form.

Who's right?  Or is this one of those things where "it depends"?
